Rallies are dirty,

This is what a La Virage evening looks like.

At some point over the weekend you deserve a comfortable chair, a cold drink and interesting people somewhere worth the photo. That's an evening. We get there first, find the place (a winery, a stone courtyard, a rooftop) and turn it into the best plan of the race weekend.

We put them on for brands, organisers and anyone bringing their people together, and as part of the weekends we sell. They bring the reason; we bring the whole evening.

03 THE HOUSE FORMAT

The night, hour by hour

HOW WE STRUCTURE AN EVENING · THE SCRIPT ADAPTS TO EACH COMMISSION
19:47

The coordinates

You get a GPS pin and a time. Nothing else. You arrive somewhere you didn't know existed (a winery, a stone courtyard) and on the door, the hairpin, engraved.

wear comfy shoes, trust us
20:30

Eight seconds of real engine

The lights go down. An engine fires up where no engine should be. Eight seconds flat out, then silence. That's all the ceremony we need: the rest of the night is yours.

21:30

The long table

One long table. Nobody at the head of it. The region writes the menu: what's eaten round here, done well. On your left a driver, on your right someone who designs helmets.

the menus come with curves
00:30

The table turns into a bar

Dinner stretches on and the table becomes a bar. Music at conversation volume; after that, we'll see. The car's still there, lit up, having its picture taken.

03:00

Nobody wants to leave

And that's exactly the measure we care about. At the door, something for your pocket: the sticker, the race number, something to remind you tomorrow that it really happened.

until the next bend

05 NOT UP FOR NEGOTIATION

Three articles. Full stop.

The bend is our heart

Others have a heart or a bear. We have the hairpin: the exact point where speed turns into skill. It's in the logo, on the stickers and in every night we'll put on.

The car is always centre stage

In every space there's a car lit like a museum piece. It isn't stuck in a corner: it's the centre of gravity of the night. We want people photographing it before they order their first drink.

No stage, no PowerPoint

At our evenings there's no stage and no PowerPoint: the brand lives in the glass, at the table and in the parting gift. The whole night is the message.
