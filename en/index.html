03 HOW WE WORK

Four steps.

1

We sit down and take stock

Of everything you have: the event or the season, the crowd, the spaces, the dates. Everything that's worth something and isn't being sold today.

2

We package it

We turn that inventory into packages a brand understands and can buy: sponsorship, hospitality, tickets, merchandising and activations.

3

We sell it and we produce it

We go out and sell it. And whatever sells, we build ourselves, so what's promised to the brand actually shows up on race weekend.

4

We get paid when you do

To start with, we work on results: we take an agreed share of what we sell.

04 THE MANIFESTO

Rallies are dirty,

loud and wonderful.
We don't want to change them.

We want them to earn what they're worth. Formula 1 knows how to sell every metre of its weekend and how to look after its people. That's the house idea: bring Formula 1's playbook to rallying, in how its assets are sold and in how its guests are welcomed.

Without washing off the mud. With a comfortable chair, a cold drink and interesting people once the last stage goes quiet.

You know where to find us. On the bend.
